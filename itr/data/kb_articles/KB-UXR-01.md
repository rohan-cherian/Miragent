## Symptom
The customer says a feature they know exists (or were told exists) isn't
visible anywhere in the product.

## Cause
Usually the feature is there but gated behind a permission the user's role
doesn't have, tucked under a menu that isn't where they expect, or only
enabled for certain plans.

## Resolution
1. Ask what they're trying to do, not just what they're looking for — the
   feature name they have in mind may not match the current UI label.
2. Check their role/permissions for that feature; a missing permission is
   the most common cause.
3. If permissions are fine, send exact navigation steps (menu path or a
   direct link) rather than a general pointer to "settings."
4. If the plan doesn't include it, say so plainly and route to the account
   team if they want to explore upgrading.

## If this doesn't work
If the navigation path has changed recently and multiple customers report
the same confusion, flag it to Product as a discoverability issue.
