## Symptom
The customer wants to know exactly how a number shown in a report or
dashboard is calculated — what's included, what's excluded, over what
period.

## Cause
Most metric definitions are documented but not surfaced next to the number
itself, so the customer has no way to see the definition without asking.

## Resolution
1. Identify the exact metric and report/view it appears in.
2. Pull the metric's definition from the reporting glossary and share it in
   plain terms, including what's included/excluded.
3. If the customer's own math doesn't match after seeing the definition,
   check the filters currently applied to their view — a mismatched filter
   is the usual cause of an apparent discrepancy.

## If this doesn't work
If the definition itself seems inconsistent with the actual displayed
number, escalate to Product Support rather than guessing at the formula.
