## Symptom
A customer writes in ahead of their renewal date asking to cancel
auto-renew, change the term length, or renegotiate pricing before the
subscription rolls over automatically.

## Cause
This isn't a defect — it's a time-sensitive request against a contract
term. The risk is entirely about timing: most auto-renew clauses have a
notice window (commonly 30 or 60 days), and once that window closes the
renewal fires automatically and reversing it becomes a much bigger
conversation involving a partial refund or an early-termination clause.

## Resolution
1. Look up the subscription's renewal date and the notice-window deadline
   in the contract terms — this determines how much time is actually left.
2. If the customer is still inside the notice window, process the
   cancellation-of-auto-renew (or term-change request) directly and confirm
   the new renewal status in writing.
3. If the request is about renegotiating price or term length rather than
   cancelling outright, route to the Renewals/CSM owner for that account —
   this is a commercial conversation, not a support toggle.
4. If the notice window has already closed, explain that clearly, state
   what options remain (e.g. cancel with the standard notice period from
   today, or an early-termination path if one exists), and do not imply the
   renewal can simply be undone after the fact.
5. Always confirm the outcome in writing with the effective date, so there
   is no ambiguity if the customer is billed again before the change lands.

## If this doesn't work
Any request involving a refund of an already-processed renewal charge, or a
term/price change outside standard published options, must go to Renewals —
support can action a clean cancellation, not a contract renegotiation.
