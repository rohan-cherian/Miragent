## Symptom
A customer asks where their business has crossed an economic nexus
threshold and whether they need to register to collect tax in a new
jurisdiction — usually prompted by growth into a new state or country, or a
notice they've received from a tax authority.

## Cause
This isn't a defect at all — it's a question the platform can help answer
with data, but the actual registration decision and filing are the
customer's (or their tax advisor's) responsibility. Support's job is to
surface the nexus dashboard accurately, not to give tax advice.

## Resolution
1. Direct the customer to the nexus/threshold monitoring dashboard for
   their account and confirm it's showing current-period data.
2. Help them read the dashboard correctly — which jurisdictions are
   approaching a threshold versus which have already crossed it.
3. Clarify explicitly that the platform tracks and reports thresholds but
   does not file registrations on the customer's behalf, and does not
   provide tax advice on when they are legally required to register.
4. If they ask whether they should register, redirect them to their tax
   advisor or the compliance team's partner referral list rather than
   answering directly.
5. If the dashboard numbers themselves look wrong (a transaction missing
   from a jurisdiction total), treat that as a data-accuracy case, not a
   nexus-advice one.

## If this doesn't work
If the customer needs a level of compliance guidance beyond what the
dashboard shows, escalate to the Compliance team — this is squarely their
domain, and giving informal tax advice from support carries real risk.
